GuitarBattle local song folder

Folder name becomes the song title: 1 Power metal

Put these files in this folder (the MIDI solo file is required):
  solo.mid       Solo guitar MIDI; GuitarBattle converts it to tablature and reads its MIDI tempo.
  drums.wav      Drum stem
  bass.wav       Bass stem
  guitar.wav     Rhythm guitar stem (not the scored solo)
  others.wav     Other instruments stem

Audio stem files may also be MP3, AIFF or FLAC. Files are optional except solo.mid.
Accepted alternative MIDI names include midi.mid, guitar_solo.mid and solo_guitar.mid.
The WAV stems should start at the same timeline origin as the MIDI. Keep the original
song tempo in the MIDI tempo map so tablature, metronome and stems remain synchronized.
